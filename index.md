## About Me
I am a senior undergraduate student in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation at [Ocean University of China (OUC)](https://english.ouc.edu.cn/) (rank: 1/71). I have been recommended for admission to the [Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)](https://english.ia.cas.cn/) as a master's student in Computer Application Technology, starting in Fall 2027. I work on embodied intelligence and robot learning, with a focus on humanoid robots. From July to October 2026, I was a research intern at RoboParty Lab, where I worked on competitive humanoid table tennis. At OUC, I work with Associate Professor Yuxi Wang and Lecturer Xiuze Xia on humanoid skill composition, and with Lecturer Haoyuan Cheng on learning-based control for bio-inspired underwater robots.

**My research goal is to build generalizable embodied agents that can perceive, reason, and act autonomously in complex real-world environments.**

## Research Interests
- **🤖 Humanoid Robot Learning:** whole-body control, reusable skill representations, and composing skills into continuous, language-driven, long-horizon execution
- **🏓 Reinforcement Learning for Interactive Tasks:** hierarchical policies and self-play that separate motor execution from strategy, as in competitive humanoid table tennis
- **🌊 Bio-inspired Robotics and Sim-to-Real:** learning-based control and autonomous obstacle avoidance for eel-like underwater robots, transferred from simulation to real water tanks
